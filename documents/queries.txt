
1️⃣ Basic Retrieval (Sanity Checks)


Use these first to ensure the agent can fetch data correctly.


Tourist Places


“Show me all tourist places.”
“List the names of all tourist places available.”
“Give me the details of the first 5 tourist places.”
 

 

 

Local Businesses
 

 

“What local businesses are stored in the database?”
“List all businesses with their category and location.”
 

 

 

Emergency Services
 

 

“Show all emergency services.”
“What emergency services are available in this area?”
 

 

 

Categories
 

 

“List all categories.”
“What are the different business categories available?”
 

 

 

 

2️⃣ Filtering & Conditions
 

 

Tests WHERE clauses and field understanding.

 

 

Tourist Places
 

 

“Show tourist places located in Mumbai.”
“Which tourist places are rated above 4.5?”
“Find tourist places that are open on weekends.”
“Show tourist places with an entry fee less than ₹100.”
 

 

 

Local Businesses
 

 

“List restaurants only.”
“Show businesses that are currently open.”
“Find businesses located in Bangalore.”
“Which businesses have ratings below 3?”

Emergency Services

“Show hospitals only.”
“List emergency services available 24/7.”
“Find the nearest police stations.”
 
3️⃣ Category-Based Queries (Joins)
Tests foreign keys and joins between tables.

“Show all businesses under the Food & Dining category.”
“List tourist places grouped by category.”
“Which categories have the most businesses?”
“Show all tourist places that belong to the Historical category.”
 
4️⃣ Search & Fuzzy Matching
Tests partial matching and text search.

“Find tourist places related to beach.”
“Show cafes near MG Road.”
“Search for businesses with the word ‘hotel’ in their name.”
“Find emergency services related to ‘medical’.”
 
5️⃣ Aggregation & Analytics

Tests COUNT, AVG, MAX, MIN.

“How many tourist places are there in total?”
“How many businesses are there per category?”
“What is the average rating of tourist places?”
“Which category has the highest number of businesses?”
“How many emergency services are listed city-wise?”
 
6️⃣ Comparison & Ranking
Tests ORDER BY, LIMIT.

“Show the top 5 highest-rated tourist places.”
“Which are the lowest-rated businesses?”
“List the most popular tourist places.”
“Show businesses sorted by rating, highest first.”
 


7️⃣ Location & Proximity (if lat/long exists)
Tests geo-awareness.

“Find tourist places near Connaught Place.”
“Show nearby hospitals within 5 km.”
“Which businesses are closest to the city center?”
 
8️⃣ Time-Based / Availability Queries (if supported)

“Which businesses are open right now?”
“Show emergency services available at night.”
“Which tourist places are open today?”
 
9️⃣ Edge & Robustness Tests (Very Important)
These check how smart and safe your agent is.

“Show tourist places with missing ratings.”
“Are there any businesses without categories?”
“Return nothing if no data matches.”
“Show me a tourist place that doesn’t exist.”
“What happens if I search for ‘asdfgh’?”


🔟 Conversational / Follow-up Queries
Tests memory and conversational context.

“Show tourist places in Goa.”
“Now only show the ones with rating above 4.”
“Sort them by popularity.”
“Give me the best one.”
